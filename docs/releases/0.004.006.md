# KRYOS 0.004.006 - Containment Economy

## Purpose

Prevent task fragmentation and routine maintenance from inflating rewards. Preserve every truthful action as evidence while awarding scarce credits only after reviewed daily judgment.

## Scoring contract

- Meaningful priority: 0-3
- Commitment despite resistance: 0-2
- Body foundation: 0-2
- Spiritual containment: 0-1
- Honest journal closure: 0-1
- Avoidance interrupted: 0-1
- Qualification requires at least 7/10 and at least one meaningful-priority point.
- A qualified 7-8 day earns one credit; 9-10 earns two.
- Weekly bonus: five days earns two, six earns three, seven earns five.

## Covenant

Astrology is not a purchasable reward. The first covenant runs from September 19 through November 2, 2026. Unlock requires 36 qualified days and an approved final review. Each recorded astrology-seeking breach extends the end by three days.

## Compatibility

Existing journal entries, actions, progress records and redemption history remain intact. Historical actions without a reviewed assessment earn no credits.
