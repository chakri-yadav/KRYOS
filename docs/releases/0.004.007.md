# KRYOS 0.004.007 - Visual Momentum

## Goal

Make honest effort immediately legible and emotionally rewarding without inventing progress or adding another data-entry burden.

## Journal

- Dedicated daily writing canvas with local draft preservation.
- At-a-glance action, domain and journal-rhythm context.
- Evidence-led timeline cards with dates, domains, completion totals and extracted records.

## Progress

- Weekly momentum command band.
- Current and personal-best streaks.
- Strongest day, leading domain, evidence days and qualified days.
- Four-week completion pulse.
- Filterable 12/52-week contribution field.
- Selected-day evidence inspector.
- Domain totals and 14-day micro-trends.

## Design constraints

- Every visual is derived from stored journal evidence.
- Empty history remains honest and visually stable.
- Charts include labels or accessible descriptions instead of relying on color alone.
- Motion remains minimal and respects reduced-motion preferences.
