# KRYOS 0.19.0 — Canonical System Design roadmap cleanup

## Summary

Keep one copy of the book-based general SDE system-design roadmap, named
`System Design Roadmap`.

## Data behavior

- The roadmap with stable ID `roadmap-system-design-book` is the keeper. Its
  modules and completed checklist items remain intact.
- Same-purpose duplicates titled `System Design Roadmap` or
  `System Design Roadmap (Book-Based)` are removed.
- Other roadmaps and Career activity history are preserved.
- The cleanup is idempotent, runs on local initialization and after cloud Career
  refresh, and persists via normal revision-checked Career sync.

## QA and limits

- Tests cover duplicate removal, unrelated roadmap preservation, retained
  progress/history, and first-time import followed by cleanup.
- No database schema change. The signed-in app must open and complete its normal
  cloud sync for the Personal account to receive the cleanup.
