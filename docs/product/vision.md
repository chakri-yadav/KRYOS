# Product Vision

KRYOS exists to help a person become consistent without losing inner honesty.

The product combines purpose, execution, habit evidence, mind containment, and progress analytics into one private system.

## Problem

The user can have strong intent but lose consistency because:

- tasks are scattered
- habits are tracked separately from execution
- planning becomes avoidance
- emotions distort the day
- progress is hard to see
- mobile capture becomes messy

## Product Thesis

Consistency improves when the system makes evidence visible and removes unnecessary decisions.

KRYOS should make the next correct action obvious, then make the pattern visible after the action.

## Target User

Initial target user:

- one serious student/developer building discipline
- wants career progress
- wants spiritual and mental alignment
- needs private journaling
- needs fast mobile capture
- needs motivating progress visuals

Future target user:

- self-directed people who want inner alignment plus execution, not just productivity.

## Success Criteria

KRYOS is working when:

- the user opens it daily
- Today becomes less messy
- habits feel motivating rather than guilt-based
- career progress is visible
- journaling reduces mental noise
- Progress shows evidence without needing extra input
- mobile is useful outside the laptop
