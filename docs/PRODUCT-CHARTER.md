# KRYOS Product Charter

## Mission

KRYOS reduces the friction between knowing and doing for one ADHD user. It externalizes memory, turns handwritten life evidence into structure, makes consistency visible, and connects meaningful effort with guilt-free enjoyment.

## Source of Truth

The journal is the primary capture layer. Reviewed assistant imports may create evidence, add persistent actions, or update existing actions. KRYOS must never invent dates, duration, completion, effort, or meaning that the journal does not support.

The personal day runs from 7:00 AM through 6:59 AM the next civil day.

## Product Areas

1. Journal: narrative evidence and observations.
2. Action Vault: the single persistent record of unfinished responsibilities.
3. Progress: visual feedback derived from journal evidence and Action Vault state.
4. Rewards: transparent effort credits exchanged for pre-approved enjoyment.

## ADHD Guardrails

- Externalize important memory.
- Limit Active work to three actions.
- Store the smallest physical next action.
- Use deadlines only when they are real.
- Preserve unfinished actions without duplicating or repeatedly carrying them forward.
- Keep advanced controls hidden until needed.
- Prefer visual evidence over guilt, warnings, or artificial urgency.
- Avoid endless feeds, variable-ratio rewards, shame, and manipulative engagement.

## Data Ownership

An action exists once. Selecting it, changing priority, waiting, activating, or completing it updates the same record. Journal completions should match an existing action by stable external ID; ambiguous matches require review.

## Product Evolution

Repeated journal patterns may justify a new feature. A one-time event does not. Before adding a substantial feature, define its unique responsibility, data owner, interaction with existing areas, and removal criteria. The user approves significant additions before publication.
